# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router), TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, deployed on Vercel from the private GitHub repo `tanayj6464/overclocked-scioly` (production branch `main`) to overclockedscioly.org. No backend yet. User approved GSAP for motion; react-bits and Vanta are optional sources the user delegated to Claude's judgement.

## Users

- Parents, relatives, and family friends deciding whether to contribute to the season, often from a shared link on a phone.
- Company sponsors and employer-matching programs checking that the team is real, organized, and properly fiscally sponsored.
- School administrators and other Science Olympiad teams checking the team's standing and results.

## Product Purpose

The public site for Overclocked, a student-led Division C Science Olympiad team in Beaverton, Oregon. It must establish the team as a credible, high-performing competitor and turn that credibility into season support: direct contributions and employer matching. Launching today, before any team photography exists.

## Positioning

A competitive team with a verified state result: 6th overall at Oregon State 2026, with medals in Bungee Drop, Helicopter, Boomilever, and Robot Tour. The team competes across all 23 Division C events. The support ask is concrete: $230 per competitor, potentially doubled by employer matching through fiscal sponsor The Hack Foundation.

## Operating Context

Support flows through Overclocked's official HCB (Hack Club Bank) fund. The Hack Foundation, a registered 501(c)(3), is the fiscal sponsor; Overclocked itself is not independently a 501(c)(3). Employer matching eligibility, limits, and procedures vary by employer.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single homepage for now: About, Results, Events, Team, Support, Employer matching, Supporters.
- All results, stats, navigation, sponsors, members, donation links, and socials live in one central config file.
- Must accept later: hero team photo, competition photos, member photos, sponsor logos, without redesign.
- Undecided / not yet provided: HCB donation URL, contact email, social links, the team logo file (user will upload), member names, sponsors.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Overclocked (formally Overclocked Science Olympiad). An existing logo exists and will be uploaded.
- Reference for polish and competitive identity: Hypernova Robotics (match level, do not copy).
- Must read as a professional, competitive, intelligent, youthful-not-childish STEM competition team. Must not read as a SaaS startup, AI-generated landing page, nonprofit template, generic school club, cyberpunk, or dark/neon.
- User-supplied palette: background #F7F8FA, text #111827, secondary #5E6675, team blue #3157D5, deep blue #17337C, gold #D3AE36 (sparingly), light blue #EEF3FF, white. Blue is the main brand color. Bright, not dark.
- No emojis, jokes, stock or AI-generated people, fake testimonials, fake dashboards, or slogans such as "empowering the next generation".

## Evidence on Hand

- 2026 Oregon State: 6th overall. 2nd Bungee Drop, 2nd Helicopter, 3rd Boomilever, 3rd Robot Tour (4 state medals).
- 23 events, 15 competitors, Division C, Beaverton, Oregon.
- Support target $230 per competitor; matching may bring a $230 contribution to $460 total.
- Absences that must not be fabricated: student names, sponsors, emails, social links, donation URLs, school approvals, any awards beyond the list above, photography.

## Product Principles

1. Results are the proof; lead with real standings, not adjectives.
2. Every support claim is exact and hedged where eligibility varies.
3. Missing material stays honestly absent and is designed to be filled later, never faked.
4. One place to update facts, so the site stays true season to season.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard navigation, semantic HTML, reduced-motion support, and responsive layouts from mobile to desktop are required.
